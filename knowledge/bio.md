# Bio & Background

Toluwalope "Tolu" Emmanuel Adegoke is a Frontend Developer based in Oyo State, Nigeria. He builds his career primarily through self-built personal products alongside his experience as an intern at FireSwitch. His core tech stack includes React, Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase, and Prisma. In his daily development workflow, he uses an AI coding agent named Antigravity as a core component.

Tolu took a self-taught path into software development, earning a freeCodeCamp Responsive Web Design certificate in 2022. He later graduated with a B.Tech in Physiology from Ladoke Akintola University of Technology in 2024.
