# Technical Writing & Personal Philosophy

Tolu writes essays and articles on his personal engineering philosophy, frontend development, software architecture, and product craftsmanship. His writing is published on his portfolio at `/blog`.

Key published articles:
1. **The Next One** (`/blog/the-next-one`):
   - A personal essay on creative philosophy and continuous growth.
   - Core premise: Inspired by an architect's response to his best design ("My next one"), Tolu views engineering not as a fixed achievement, but as an evolving journey where every project must surpass the last. His past work marks lessons learned, his present work reflects current capabilities, and his next project holds the discovery of his full potential.
